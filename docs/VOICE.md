# Azza voice: learning first

Azza helps adults learn real skills properly, in Arabic or English, guided by a real teacher. Live sessions are one of the ways that happens; they are not the product. The product is **the learning, and the skill you leave with**.

## The shift

| Before (live-first) | After (learning-first) |
|---|---|
| "Learn live. Real teacher. Small class." | "Learn it. Practise it. Make it yours." |
| Seats, rooms, "on air", doors opening | Progress, practice, feedback, mastery |
| Batch = the thing you buy | The course = what you learn; the class = who you learn with |
| Session recordings as a fallback | Lessons, practice, live help and recordings as one path |
| Theatre / broadcast metaphors | A guided path: step, practice, feedback, proof |

## What we promise
These are all true in the product today:
1. **A clear path.** Each course is broken into modules and lessons you can follow step by step.
2. **Practice that sticks.** Quizzes and assignments after the lessons, with feedback.
3. **A teacher who guides you.** Live sessions to ask, see examples and get unstuck. Your teacher knows your name because classes stay small.
4. **Your pace between sessions.** Lessons and every session's recording are in your library.
5. **Proof you learned it.** A certificate anyone can verify.
6. **In Arabic or English.**

Never promise what isn't true: no "learn anything", no job guarantees, no invented stats, no "self-paced" (there is a schedule).

## Tone
- **Calm, encouraging and specific.** Talk to one person ("you"). Short sentences, active verbs.
- **Lead with the learner's outcome**, then how we get them there.
- **Keep ops copy plain.** Payments, errors and admin should be clear and literal, not playful. Retire the broadcast jokes there ("Something went off air", "This take doesn't exist", "Not your studio") in favour of plain friendly copy.
- **Arabic is written natively,** in Modern Standard Arabic that's warm but not formal. It's never a word-for-word translation. Use Western digits. The brand name is عازة.

## Key lines
- **Hero:** "Learn it. Practise it. Make it yours." / «تعلّمها. تدرّب عليها. أتقِنها.»
- **Support line:** "Step-by-step courses in Arabic and English, with lessons, practice and a teacher who guides you until the skill is yours." / «دورات خطوة بخطوة بالعربية والإنجليزية: دروس وتدريب ومعلّم يرشدك حتى تُتقن المهارة.»
- **Primary CTA:** "Find your course" / «اعثر على دورتك»
- **Secondary CTA:** "See how it works" / «كيف نتعلّم»

## Landing story (copy only; keep the sections and their motion)
1. **Hero:** the promise above. Kicker: "Courses in Arabic & English".
2. **Small classes** (the seat grid): "A teacher who knows your name." Classes stay small so you get feedback, not a broadcast. The seat counts stay, framed as "places left in this class".
3. **Your week** (calendar): "Learning that fits your week." Lessons any time, plus one or two live sessions at a set time to practise with your teacher.
4. **The lime band** (three words): **Learn · Practise · Master** (تعلّم · تدرّب · أتقِن). One line each: lessons step by step; quizzes and assignments with feedback; a certificate that proves it.
5. **In the room** (live): "Stuck? Ask." Live sessions are where you ask questions, see worked examples and get unstuck.
6. **Recordings:** "Learn at your pace." Every lesson and session recording stays in your library, so you can rewatch or catch up.
7. **Catalog:** "Find what you want to learn."
8. **Starting soon:** "New classes starting soon."
9. **Teachers:** "Learn from people who do it for a living."
10. **Certificate:** "Proof you learned it."
11. **Teach:** "Teach what you know."
12. **FAQ and final CTA:** "Your next skill starts here."

## Product surfaces to update
- **Meta and SEO:** title "Azza — Learn real skills in Arabic and English", description from the support line.
- **Auth:** sign-in "Welcome back. Pick up where you left off."; sign-up "Start learning today." The panel shows the three promises: path, practice, proof.
- **Mobile:** splash tagline, onboarding pages (learn → practise → get help → prove it), and empty states ("No lessons yet…").
- **Learner dashboard / home:** next lesson, practice due, progress. Live sessions appear as one item among them.
- **Course page:** "What you'll learn" first. "Choose your class" instead of "Pick your week / choose your batch". Session counts are a detail, not the headline.
